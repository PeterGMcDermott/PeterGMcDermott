# Peter McDermott

I lead AI and automation adoption at Marriott. I also write AI Ayo, a weekly newsletter that translates AI news for normal humans, and I host the Going Up podcast.

This is where my experiments live. Most of them started as something I built with my AI assistant and then kept working on.

## Experiments

- [tsa-throughput-dashboard](https://github.com/PeterGMcDermott/tsa-throughput-dashboard): three years of TSA checkpoint screening data, per airport. Built because I wanted to know which airports are actually growing.
- [reddit-ai-monitor](https://github.com/PeterGMcDermott/reddit-ai-monitor): a prototype bot that watches Reddit for posts mentioning a brand and an AI topic.
- [bonvoy-passport](https://github.com/PeterGMcDermott/bonvoy-passport): a stamp-style tracker for all 40 Marriott Bonvoy brands.
- [reading-punchcard](https://github.com/PeterGMcDermott/reading-punchcard): a punch card app so my son can track the books he reads toward his next Among Us plushie.
- [music-theory-synthesizer](https://github.com/PeterGMcDermott/music-theory-synthesizer): a Duolingo-style synth studio for learning music theory and building beats, with MP3 export.
- [chord-progression-explorer](https://github.com/PeterGMcDermott/chord-progression-explorer): an interactive chord progression explorer for learning songwriting, with WAV loop export.
- [corporate-jargon-trainer](https://github.com/PeterGMcDermott/corporate-jargon-trainer): Duolingo for corporate jargon, a plainspoken leadership masterclass.
- [corporate-ai-digestive-system](https://github.com/PeterGMcDermott/corporate-ai-digestive-system): a satirical decision game about how enterprises digest AI strategy.
- [who-farted](https://github.com/PeterGMcDermott/who-farted): a playable prototype of an Among Us-style deduction game.
